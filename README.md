# Project Idea

An Upwork-style platform, but focused on education.

## Student Side

Students can browse available courses and events, organized by categories or subjects. They can open a course page (for example, a Python class or a high school math masterclass), learn more about it, and visit the teacher's profile to see their past and upcoming courses.

To reserve a spot, students create an account and complete the registration process. They get access to notifications, a built-in group chat, and all the event details, including either a video call link for online sessions or the location for in-person events.

## Teacher Side

Teachers can also browse the platform, but they have access to a dashboard where they can create, edit, and manage their profile, courses, and events. They can upload banners, videos, and other promotional content.

They also have a secure built-in chat and a notification system that lets them approve or reject student requests to join their courses or events.

+ an agency like feature for groups of teachers ( like upworks agency one )

## Business Model

The platform is free for teachers until they start earning money through it. We don't charge anything while they're still getting started.

Payments can either be handled securely through the platform or made directly to the teacher, depending on what works best.

The goal is to build trust first. For example, if a teacher only has a small number of students, they pay nothing. Once they begin attracting more clients and generating consistent income, they move to a subscription plan. The subscription price scales with their success, so teachers who earn more contribute more to the platform.
